WHAT WE DO

Our primary solution is The SIMRP (Self-Insured Medical Reimbursement Plan), an IRS-authorized tax strategy that redirects existing FICA payroll taxes into supplemental employee benefits.

We also offer a full suite of supplemental insurance products through top carriers.

PRIMARY SOLUTION: THE SIMRP (INFINITE SAVINGS PLAN)
-----------------------------------------------------
The SIMRP is not insurance — it is an IRS-authorized tax strategy that funds employee benefits using dollars already leaving your payroll. See the SIMRP Knowledge Base for full details.

Key numbers to remember:
- Employers save ~$550 per enrolled employee per year in payroll taxes
- Employees receive $125–$150/month in supplemental benefits at zero change to take-home pay
- 0 new cost to the employer
- 5-step implementation (Census → Proposal → Enrollment → Payroll Integration → Savings)
- Compliant with IRS §125, §105(b), §106(a), §1.105-11, ADA, ERISA, HIPAA

SUPPLEMENTAL BENEFITS CATALOG
------------------------------
LH Capital agents offer the following supplemental insurance products:

1. CHRONIC ILLNESS COVERAGE
   - Provides cash benefits when diagnosed with a qualifying chronic condition
   - Covers ongoing care, home health, and assisted living costs
   - Benefits paid directly to policyholder

2. CRITICAL ILLNESS COVERAGE
   - Lump-sum cash payment upon diagnosis of heart attack, stroke, cancer, kidney failure, and other critical conditions
   - Use funds for medical bills, lost wages, childcare, or any expense
   - Complements existing health insurance

3. SHORT-TERM DISABILITY INSURANCE
   - Replaces a portion of income if unable to work due to illness or injury
   - Typical benefit: 40–70% of weekly gross income
   - Benefit periods: 13, 26, or 52 weeks depending on plan

4. ACCIDENT INSURANCE
   - Cash benefits for injuries from covered accidents (emergency room, hospitalization, fractures, etc.)
   - No restrictions on how benefits are spent
   - Covers off-the-job accidents not covered by workers' comp

5. HOSPITAL INDEMNITY INSURANCE
   - Fixed cash payment per day of hospitalization
   - Helps cover out-of-pocket costs your health insurance doesn't
   - Benefit paid regardless of other coverage

6. LIFE INSURANCE (TERM & WHOLE)
   - Affordable term life insurance for employees and their families
   - Whole life options with living benefits (chronic/critical illness riders)
   - Group and individual options available

7. CANCER INSURANCE
   - Specific-disease coverage that pays cash benefits upon cancer diagnosis
   - Covers initial diagnosis, treatment, surgery, radiation, and recovery
   - Critical gap-filler alongside major medical coverage

8. LONG-TERM CARE INSURANCE
   - Covers extended care services when unable to perform daily living activities
   - Covers nursing homes, assisted living, in-home care
   - Protects retirement savings from being depleted by care costs

- Average employer savings: ~$550 per enrolled employee per year

- Zero change to employee take-home pay
- FICA tax rate: 7.65% (employer pays 7.65% on wages; redirected dollars reduce this burden)
